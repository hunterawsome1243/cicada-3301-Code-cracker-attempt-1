## Bootstrap checklist: what the prompts assume exists

The four files name support artifacts that the spec's Phase 1 sessions must create before the loop can run as written; building them is the first interactive session's work, and until they exist the prompts fail loudly (trust anchor red, missing script) rather than silently. They are: the frozen-toolbox list `FROZEN.txt` and the two hook scripts `scripts/hooks/deny_frozen.sh` and `scripts/hooks/stop_gate.sh` (specified in OVERNIGHT_LOOP.md Part 1 §6), registered in `.claude/settings.json`; the gates `scripts/check_results.py` (murphy's field rules), `scripts/validate_ledger.py` (Dukotah's six rules) and `scripts/threshold_for.py` (the Gumbel fit from two observed maxima, per decoder preset and segment length); the CLI verbs the prompts call, `runeforge canon --verify`, `runeforge status [--short|--round]`, `runeforge ledger append|update`, `runeforge gate`, `runeforge control`, `runeforge sweep --detach` (which must write `runs/<id>/sweep.pid` and a `status.json` carrying `updated_epoch`, `deadline_epoch`, `done`, `kill_condition` and the resume cursor), `runeforge crosser replay|reencipher`, and `python -m runeforge.oracle --selftest|--key-module`; the state files `ledger/ledger.jsonl`, `queue.json`, `PICKUP-HERE.md` (with `Rejections` and `Lessons` sections), `TOOLBOX_REQUESTS.md` and the `runs/overnight/` directory; the subagent `.claude/agents/verify-lane.md` (fresh context, `Read` and `Bash(python *)` only, a small model is fine) and the path-scoped rules `.claude/rules/gpu.md`, `lanes.md`, `reports.md` holding the PREREG and RESULTS templates copied from Dukotah's round-18 and round-24 lanes. Installing the three procedure files as skills (`.claude/skills/session/SKILL.md` and so on) lets Hunter type `/session`, `/overnight`, `/hit` instead of pasting, and `disable-model-invocation: true` on them keeps them out of context until invoked ([Context window](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/context-window)). Finally, `/doctor prompt-audit` after any edit to `CLAUDE.md`, and `/context` once to confirm which memory files actually loaded ([Memory docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/memory)).
